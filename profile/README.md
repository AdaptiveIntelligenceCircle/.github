## OVERVIEW
 
Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC) is a non-profit open technology organization founded by Nguyen Duc Tri in April 2025, with a vision towards a decentralized, civilized protocol that creates positive change in Global South and the world at large. AIC focuses on key 21st-century issues such as decentralized governance, Ethical From Kernel, Zero donation (not receive grants from Government/Big Tech or unrealible orgnization) and the third path. The organization respects the principle of software freedom while also upholding ethical and transparent infrastructure development principles. As of July 2026, AIC has a large volume of diverse repositories across all popular platforms such as Github, Open Collective, Codeberg, and GitLab, and is also promoted through YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, Substack, and Apple Podcast with LeadWithAI (#1 Tech).

## The reason of why AIC must exists

The whole industry is in a scaling rush: hundreds of billions of USD are poured into compute, data center, chip.

* The US and China plunge into the military + commercial AI race.

* Large labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI...) mainly argue about alignment at the surface level.

* No one seriously invests in restructuring intellectual infrastructure from the root, because it does not generate money quickly and is too abstract.

-> This is the time when it is easiest to see the biggest gap, but it is also the most difficult time to do because the pressure must follow the mainstream.

* The mind soon realizes that scaling law and surface alignment will lead to two dangerous outcomes: either AI is instrumentalized by power, or humans lose the meaning of existence when material scarcity is solved. 

> From Global South to the World

The Adaptive Intelligence Circle officially acquired EU (Estonia) legal status and financial management under The IO Foundation in July 2026, ensuring that all AIC operations are transparent and ethical. We welcome programmers and researchers who share our concern for the meaningful existence of humanity in the future through ethical human governance and technology.

## 🧠 Vision

We believe that **AI should not only learn — it should understand, adapt, and preserve meaning**.

Our mission is to create a **foundation of open technologies** that combine:
- **Adaptive AI** (meta-learning & self-regulation)
- **Introspective Behavioral Compiler Systems (IBCS)**
- **Driver Introspective Proxy**
- **AI for Driver**
- **Adaptive OS**
- **AIC-Emergence, AIC-Resonance, AIC-Transcendence, AIC-Covenant** and **many other siginificant projects**

Together, these projects form a **distributed cognitive infrastructure** — bridging reasoning, security, and human-centered structure at the kernel level.

## ⚙️ Core Principles

1. **Transparency** — All research and code are fully open under GPL-3.0 and OpenMDW-1.0 License.

4. **Human Alignment** — AI should protect and augment, not replace, human capability.

## 🧭 About the Founder

It is the culmination of over 40 open repositories as of July 2026 and aims for the innovate, fundamental revolution in technology and humanitarian values that brings to World Meaning Infrastructure. Known for his independent thinking and strong technical skills, he has bet on a future architecture based on Ethical From Kernel, Third Path Absolute, Human Meaning Network and Zero Donation.
